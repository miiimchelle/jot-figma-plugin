# Jot v2.1.0

Notes now live on the canvas. Every linked note becomes a sticky next to its layer, colour coded by tag, and the plugin has a Figma-native look with rich text notes.

## Highlights

- **Stickies on the canvas.** Each linked note is drawn as a sticky beside its layer: blue for Decision, amber for Assumption, purple for Trade-off, green for Feedback, red for Design debt, grey for no tag. Several notes on one layer stack neatly in a column.
- **Rich text notes.** Bold, italic, bullet and numbered lists, and links. Formatting shows in Jot and on the sticky.
- **Figma-native UI.** Figma's own tabs, dropdowns, fields and buttons, in light and dark mode to match Figma.
- **Sign your notes.** Choose whether stickies show your avatar, name and timestamp. Jot remembers your choice.
- **Link, relink, unlink.** Link a note to the selected layer, move it to another layer, or unlink it and keep the note.

## Also new

- Optional heading for each note
- Tags are optional, with a "No tag" filter
- "Export markdown" sits next to the filter; the Entries list scrolls while the filter stays put
- Save is pinned to the bottom of the Write tab and the Note editor fills the space above it
- Links in notes open in your browser

## Fixes

- Jumping to a linked layer on another page works again
- Notes and layer names can no longer inject HTML into the plugin
- The plugin UI renders correctly in Figma (styles no longer clash)
- Jot follows Figma's theme, including dark mode

## Good to know

- Jot now asks for your Figma name and photo (`currentuser` permission) to sign stickies, and loads your photo from `*.figma.com`. Turn off **Avatar** to skip that request. No note content leaves the file.
- Existing notes keep working. Their stickies appear, or pick up the new colours and formatting, the next time you edit or link the note.
- Settings moved from the gear button to its own **Settings** tab.
